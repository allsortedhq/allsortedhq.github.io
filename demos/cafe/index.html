Our story

A neighbourhood café with a roasting habit

Harbour & Roast started as a tiny espresso cart and grew into Millbrook’s go-to for specialty coffee and unhurried brunch. We roast small batches weekly, bake our own pastries, and keep the playlist low enough for a proper conversation.

Whether you’re grabbing a flat white to go or settling in with friends, you’re welcome here.
