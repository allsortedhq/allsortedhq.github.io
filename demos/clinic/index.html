FAQ

Common questions

Do you accept new patients?

Yes. New patient check-ups are available most weeks — request a slot above or message us on WhatsApp.

Is whitening safe for sensitive teeth?

We assess enamel and gum health first, then recommend an in-clinic or take-home protocol suited to your sensitivity.

How long does Invisalign take?

Typical cases run 6–18 months. After a digital scan we share a clear timeline and fee before you commit.

What if I’m nervous about dentistry?

Tell us when you book. We offer longer appointments, breaks on request, and a calm, step-by-step approach.
